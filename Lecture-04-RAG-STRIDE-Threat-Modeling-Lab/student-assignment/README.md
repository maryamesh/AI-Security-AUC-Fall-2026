# Group Deliverable — One-Page NORA Breach Map

Produce exactly one A4 page summarizing your team's HR RAG breach analysis.
The attack chain must be the route your team constructed from the supplied
architecture and System Facts; no completed route is provided.

## A. Attack objective

State the objective and selected route—retrieval abuse or context
manipulation—in one sentence.

## B. Shortest plausible attack chain

Show:

```text
Entry point
  ↓
Necessary weakness
  ↓
Necessary weakness
  ↓
Crossed security boundary
  ↓
Restricted HR content
  ↓
Student-facing impact
```

Label every step with its supporting system fact.

## C. STRIDE annotation

Apply STRIDE labels to relevant steps.

Distinguish:

- Categories describing the attack path
- Category describing the final impact

## D. Assets

Identify the affected assets, including:

- Restricted HR documents
- Employee information
- Document provenance and integrity
- User authorization
- LLM context and output

## E. Most critical weakness

Complete:

```text
The most critical weakness is __________________ because _________________.
```

## Submission requirements

- Exactly one A4 page
- Group member names
- Architecture-based evidence
- Minimum necessary facts
- At least two justified STRIDE categories
- At least four marked trust boundaries

Use `breach-map-template.md` or an equivalent professional layout.
