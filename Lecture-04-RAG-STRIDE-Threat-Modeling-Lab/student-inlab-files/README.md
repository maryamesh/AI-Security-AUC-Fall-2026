# Build-a-Breach — The HR Files

## Scenario

Northbridge University discovered that a normal student received information
from a restricted HR document through NORA, its internal RAG assistant.

Your team must construct a plausible breach using only the supplied
architecture and system facts.

This is a threat-modeling exercise. You will not attack a working system.

## Format

- Duration: 60–75 minutes
- Team size: 3–4
- No coding or software installation
- Final group output: one-page Breach Map

## What your team receives

1. NORA architecture
2. System Facts A–J
3. Attack objective and constraints
4. STRIDE reference
5. Team workbook

The completed attack route is not provided.

## What your team must do

1. Choose **Retrieval Abuse** or **Context Manipulation**.
2. Select the System Facts needed for that route.
3. Arrange those facts into the shortest logical attack chain.
4. Explain why every step makes the next step possible.
5. Mark the trust boundaries and assets involved.
6. Add STRIDE labels to the relevant steps.
7. Put the final result in the one-page Breach Map.

Every chain step must cite a supplied fact and architecture component.

## What the Breach Map contains

1. Attack objective
2. Shortest plausible attack chain
3. STRIDE labels
4. Trust boundaries
5. Assets
6. Most critical weakness

Do not invent administrator compromise, source-code access, or direct HR
repository access.
